Misingi Online Discipleship Program

Misingi is a structured online discipleship program that offers a clear, chronological overview of the Bible—from Genesis to the ascension of Christ. Over 12 online sessions, you'll explore 50 foundational lessons designed to strengthen your faith, deepen your understanding of core Christian doctrines, and reveal the promise and purpose of Jesus Christ. This program equips you to recognize scriptural errors, embrace biblical truth, and see the unity of the entire Bible—helping you grow spiritually wherever you are.
